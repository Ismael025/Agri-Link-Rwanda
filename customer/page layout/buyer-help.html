Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse the marketplace, open a product, choose a valid quantity, add it to your cart, confirm delivery and complete checkout.

How do I know a farmer is verified?

Verified farmers display a verification label. Review their profile, ratings, location and completed orders before purchasing.

Can I cancel an order?

Cancellation depends on the current order stage. Open the order or contact support before preparation or delivery has progressed too far.

How do I track delivery?

Open Track Delivery and choose the active order to view status, estimated arrival, driver information and delivery history.

How are refunds handled?

Approved refunds return through the original supported payment method according to platform rules and payment-provider processing time.

How do I report a product problem?

Contact support with the order number, a clear explanation and relevant evidence. Never share passwords or verification codes.
