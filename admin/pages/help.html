Frequently Asked Questions

Who can create an administrator account?

Administrator accounts are created privately by an authorized super administrator. They are not available through public registration.

What should be checked before farmer approval?

Confirm identity, district, contact information, business or cooperative documents, and any risk indicators.

Can an admin edit a completed payment?

No. Financial records should remain immutable. Corrections should use a documented refund or adjustment process.

Why are actions recorded in audit logs?

Audit logs provide accountability, security monitoring and traceability for sensitive platform actions.
