Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new product?

Open My Products, select Add New Product, complete all required information, upload clear product images and submit it for approval.

Why is my product pending approval?

Agri-Link reviews new listings to confirm that information is complete and appropriate. You will receive a notification after review.

How do I accept and prepare an order?

Open Orders, review the buyer, quantity, price and delivery information, then choose Accept. Update the order as it moves through preparation and delivery.

When does money appear in my wallet?

Payments appear as pending while an order is being processed. They become available according to the platform payment and delivery confirmation rules.

How can I compare market prices?

Use Market Prices to compare the current price, weekly average, highest and lowest price across supported districts.

How do I report a problem with an order?

Use the support form, include the order number and explain the issue clearly. Do not share passwords or verification codes.
